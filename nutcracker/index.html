The Nutcracker

Auditions, placement and performance information.

di.

Dance Institute’s Nutcracker brings dancers together for ballet, rehearsals and performance. Contact the studio for this season’s audition details, rehearsal times and fees.

Who can audition?
The studio’s published audition information welcomes dancers ages eight and older.
What happens?
Dancers learn a short ballet routine and perform for the teaching panel, individually or in small groups. These are placement auditions to assign roles.
What should I wear?
Arrive in fitted dance clothing with hair secured. Ask the studio if you need help preparing.
Rehearsals & registration
Rehearsal commitments depend on the role. Confirm the current schedule with the studio and look for registration under Camps/Events in Sawyer.
