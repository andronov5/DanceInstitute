Miss Michele

Michele began dancing at three and assisting with classes at fourteen. Her teaching combines technique with a playful approach for each age group.

Training & experience

At the University of Minnesota, Michele performed, choreographed and assisted with tap and ballroom courses. Her training includes ballet, tap, jazz, hip-hop, modern, tumbling and ballroom.
