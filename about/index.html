About the studio

Studio history, teaching approach and community.

Dance Institute brings technical dance training into a friendly, encouraging studio. Our goal is a well-rounded dance education that builds confidence, poise and a lasting love of movement.

A little of everything
Combination classes teach several dance styles in one class, leaving room for other interests.
Parents are part of it
Watch from the designated viewing area and connect through the Dance Institute Parent Organization.
Connected to our community
Students perform at community events, retirement homes and the annual June recital.
A year of progress
The school year runs from September through June. Summer workshops give new and returning dancers more time in the studio.
