Studio policies

Clothing, shoes, hair and classroom guidelines.

di.

Before class

Bring the right dance shoes and a closed water bottle. Dancewear is available through the studio.

Clothing
The studio lists black short-sleeve leotards and pink tights for girls through age nine. Leotards and tights are the base attire; ballet skirts and dance pants for jazz, tap and hip-hop are welcome. Boys may wear dance pants and a T-shirt. Clothing should allow movement without being loose or baggy.
Hair
Keep hair away from the face. A bun works well for long hair; pigtails are recommended for tumbling.
In the studio
Follow your teacher’s directions. No hanging on ballet barres, chewing gum or wearing jewelry. Unsafe behavior may mean sitting out.
Watching class
Parents are welcome in the viewing area. Please keep conversations outside the class.
