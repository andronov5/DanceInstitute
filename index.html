A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

BEFORE
THE first beat.

What if we’ve never danced before?+

Everyone has a first class. Start with the age filters above, then call the studio to choose an appropriate level. Pointe and advanced classes require placement guidance.

Can parents watch class?+

Yes. Parents can watch from the designated viewing area and participate in the studio’s parent community.

Where can I see times and tuition?+

Check the studio schedule for class times and Sawyer for membership options. Confirm current tuition and availability before registering.

What should I wear or bring?+

Clothing and shoes depend on the class. Call (303) 525-0011 before your first visit so the studio can help you arrive prepared.

How does registration work?+

Registration is handled through Sawyer. Choose a membership, select “Learn More,” and follow the registration instructions. Need a hand? Call the studio.
