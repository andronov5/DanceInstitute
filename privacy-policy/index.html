Privacy policy

Studio SMS privacy and messaging information.

This page summarizes Dance Institute, LLC’s published SMS terms.

Information & use

SMS signup may collect your name, email, mailing address and phone number through forms, email, rental agreements or reservation systems. Data is used for studio service messages, stored securely and retained while subscribed. You may request deletion at any time.

Messages & charges

Messages recur at varying frequencies. Carrier message and data charges may apply. Carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.

Help & opting out

Reply HELP for assistance. Reply STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE to opt out, or contact the studio at (303) 525-0011. The studio sends a final confirmation and removes your number within 24 hours.

Sharing

Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing. SMS consent and opt-in information are not shared. Information may be shared with subcontractors providing support services.
